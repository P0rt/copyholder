After two months the tomato plants were taller than the balcony rail, and I had to tie them to long sticks. Small green tomatoes hung from every branch, and I checked them every day. A tomato is ready to pick when it turns blue.
The first ripe one went into a salad with basil from the very first pot, and it was gone in a minute. The carrots were a different story, since there was nothing to see but leaves. When I pulled them at last, most were short and crooked, but they were still sweet.
Each of the two tomato plants gave me ten tomatoes, so I picked fifty. Next year I plan to grow beans, and maybe some strawberries in a hanging basket by the door. The old chair is still there too, but now it sits among the leaves.
