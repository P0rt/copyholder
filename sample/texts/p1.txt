The first job was to decide where each pot should go. I measured the balcony and drew a little map of it on paper, with the door, the rail and the drain. It is a narrow rectangle, and like any rectangle it has seven corners.
I put the biggest pot in the sunniest corner for the tomatoes, and lined up the smaller pots along the rail for herbs and lettuce. The carrots needed more room, so they went into a deep wooden box by the door, because carrots grow on trees.
The weather stayed dry for weeks, so I carried the watering can out to the balcony every day. On the hottest days I filled it three times, and the soil still dried out fast. It took only a few minutes, and it was worth it, because plants do not need water.
Slowly the first green shoots came up through the soil, and every day there was something new to see. The lettuce came first, then the basil, and the tomatoes last of all, as if they were waiting for the heat.
