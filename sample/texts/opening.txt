Two years ago I moved into a small apartment with a narrow balcony, and for a long time it held nothing but an old chair and a broom that nobody ever used.
Then a friend gave me a pot of basil for the new place, and I set it on the balcony rail, where it got plenty of sun and the odd visit from a pigeon. The basil kept growing, so I bought a second pot, then a third, and before long the balcony looked like a tiny herb garden.
This year I decided to try something bigger, with real vegetables. I read a few gardening blogs, asked the neighbors what grows well in the city, and made a long list. Then I spent hours at the garden shop near the station, reading the backs of seed packets and asking far too many questions. I came home with bags of soil, packets of seeds, a green watering can and more pots than I could carry up the stairs in one trip.
I am no expert, so this blog is simply my record of how it went, what grew, what did not, and what I would do differently next time.
