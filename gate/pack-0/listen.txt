Long ago, a village well was dug by hand. Two workers took turns with a spade, lifting earth into a bucket on a rope. As the hole grew deeper, they lined its sides with flat stones, so the walls would not fall in. After many days, the ground at the bottom turned damp, and then wet. A wooden roof was built over the top, with a wheel to lift the bucket. Every morning, people from the nearby houses came with jugs and pails. The well was a good place to stop and share the news.
